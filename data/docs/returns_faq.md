# Returns FAQ

**How long do I have to return an item?**
Customers can request a refund within 30 days of delivery. The item must be unused and in its original packaging.

**How do I start a return?**
Open your order in the account area and choose "Return item". Print the prepaid label and drop the parcel at any partner post office.

**Can I exchange instead of returning?**
Exchanges for a different size or colour are free within 30 days, subject to stock.
